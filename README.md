Welcome to the official resource repository of **Mukabbir University of Science & Technology** (Gujrat). Here you can explore overviews, curriculum highlights, and career pathways for our academic programs.

### Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm-D)
The 5-year (10-semester) Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm-D) program prepares future healthcare leaders with strong clinical skills, pharmacological expertise, and industrial knowledge. Blending classroom instruction with laboratory training and hands-on hospital practice, the curriculum covers pharmaceutics, pharmacology, medicinal chemistry, clinical pharmacy, and drug regulatory affairs.

👉 **[Explore Pharm-D Program & Admissions]((https://mukabbiruniversity.edu.pk/))**

---

### BS Computer Science (BSCS)
The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) program combines strong theoretical foundations with hands-on experience in modern computing technologies. With a focus on Artificial Intelligence, software development, data science, and ethical computing, students train in advanced labs using real-world tools.

👉 **[Explore BS Computer Science (BSCS) Program][(https://mukabbiruniversity.edu.pk/]**
